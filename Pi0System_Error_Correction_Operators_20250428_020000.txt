1. QUANTUM ERROR CORRECTION OPERATORS
----------------------------------
1.1 Surface Code Implementation
The primary stabilizer Hamiltonian:
$$ H_{stabilizer} = -\sum_s A_s - \sum_p B_p $$
Where:
$$ A_s = \prod_{i \in s} \sigma^x_i $$
$$ B_p = \prod_{i \in p} \sigma^z_i $$

1.2 Error Detection Operators
$$ E_{detect} = \sum_i (\sigma^x_i + \sigma^y_i + \sigma^z_i) $$
Error syndrome measurement:
$$ S_j = \prod_{i \in C_j} \sigma^\alpha_i $$

2. PROCESS HANDLING GENERATORS
----------------------------
2.1 Quantum State Generator
$$ |\psi\rangle = \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \alpha_i |i\rangle $$
With normalization constraint:
$$ \sum_i |\alpha_i|^2 = 1 $$

2.2 Process Evolution Generator
$$ U(t) = \exp(-iHt/\hbar) $$
Where H is the system Hamiltonian:
$$ H = H_{system} + H_{control} + H_{environment} $$

4. MATHEMATICAL FRAMEWORK
----------------------
4.1 Error Channel Representation
Quantum channel description:
$$ \mathcal{E}(\rho) = \sum_k E_k \rho E_k^\dagger $$
With Kraus operators satisfying:
$$ \sum_k E_k^\dagger E_k = I $$

4.2 Recovery Operations
Recovery channel:
$$ \mathcal{R}(\rho) = \sum_j R_j \rho R_j^\dagger $$
Fidelity metric:
$$ F = \sqrt{\langle\psi|\mathcal{R}(\mathcal{E}(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|))|\psi\rangle} $$

5. ADVANCED FEATURES
------------------
5.1 Adaptive Error Correction
Dynamic threshold adjustment:
$$ \tau_{new} = \tau_{old} \cdot \exp(-\gamma \epsilon) $$
Where $$\epsilon$$ is the error rate and $$\gamma$$ is the learning rate.

5.2 Process Optimization
Quantum process fidelity:
$$ F_{process} = \frac{|Tr(U_{ideal}^\dagger U_{actual})|^2}{d^2} $$
Where d is the dimension of the Hilbert space.
